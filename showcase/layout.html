12 .juno-canvas-ink — marks over arbitrary imagery (ink + halo, legible at every luminance)

The backing runs black → white on purpose. The worst case is the middle, not the ends: there both halves of the pair are weakest at once. Over a light backing the halo carries the contrast; over a dark one the ink does.

The bar along the bottom is .juno-canvas-scrim — chrome floating over live content. It is deliberately not --juno-opacity-scrim: that value suppresses a modal's background, and here the background is the thing being annotated, so greying it out would defeat the purpose. Under prefers-reduced-transparency it drops the blur for the opaque surface, because a translucent bar over moving content is the case where that preference matters most.

13 .juno-rail--responsive / .juno-dock--responsive — the swap is exhaustive: exactly one shows, ever

Real rule: (pointer: coarse) and ((width <= 767.98px) or (height <= 500px)) — see dock-responsive.md. The pair lives in an <iframe>, not inline: a media query reads the enclosing viewport, and only an iframe has one of its own that a resizable wrapper can actually change — a plain resizable <div> cannot, its own resizing is invisible to the rule. Drag the frame's corner to test the width/height half for real. A mouse never matches pointer: coarse on its own — the demo's own preview toggle (inside the frame) stands in for that half.
