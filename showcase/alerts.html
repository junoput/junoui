Alerts & toasts

Say it once, in the right color.

Inline alerts carry in-context messages; toasts float transient confirmations on the reserved alert layer. Both read var(--juno-role) set by a .juno--<role> class — color reinforces the icon + word, never replaces it. junoui ships the look + motion; the app owns dismiss and timing.
