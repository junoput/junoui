RG .juno-range — dual-thumb slider: two sliders in a group, each announcing the other as its limit

The second pair is 4% apart: their tap boxes already overlap while the 16px grips are still two dots — the overlap is in the hit areas, not the paint. How wide that band is depends on the track's width, which is why the grip is smaller than its box rather than a fixed gap. The third pair is exactly coincident, where the grips necessarily coincide too; that is the case nearest-centre cannot answer and the direction rule does (junoui/range::pickThumb).
