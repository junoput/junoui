Safe-area insets — env(safe-area-inset-*)

WHATVALUEVERDICT

Resolved by measuring a probe element whose four paddings are the four env() values. All-zero on a phone in portrait means viewport-fit=cover did not take — nothing else on this page is trustworthy. Portrait and landscape differ: portrait puts the inset on top/bottom, landscape moves it to left/right.
