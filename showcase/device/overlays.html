03 · Drawers — one per edge

Side drawers cap at min(380px, 85vw) on narrow viewports so a sliver of scrim survives as the "tap outside to close" target — check that sliver exists. In landscape the drawer must not slide under the notch.

04 · Popover API — top layer, never clipped

Popover, menu and tooltip render in the browser top layer. These sit inside a card with overflow: hidden on purpose: if WebKit clips them at the card edge, the top layer is not working.
