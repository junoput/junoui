The focus-zoom experiment iOS Safari is widely observed to zoom the page onto a focused text field whose font-size is under 16px. junoui holds text entry at a 16px floor on coarse pointers to stop that. No primary Apple source exists for the behaviour — so measure it here, on the device, instead of citing folklore.
