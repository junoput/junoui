What is on this screen

Five pillbars at once, because they occupy five different anchors: the centered bar at the foot (sticky, in flow), and one fixed cluster in each corner. The top-right cluster deliberately overlaps this harness's own top bar — that is what a fixed corner pill does in a real app, and it is how you can see the top inset being honoured.

  • Corner variants clamp themselves with env(safe-area-inset-*) on both axes — rotate to landscape and confirm they step away from the notch side.
  • The bottom pair must clear the home indicator; the centered bar rides 16px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom) above the foot (addition, not max()).
  • Tap the __overflow "more" button top-right: the menu is a native popover in the top layer. It must not be clipped by anything.
  • Tap the search field in the centered bar. The page must not zoom. The input is held at a 16px floor precisely to stop iOS focus zoom — if the page scales, that floor broke.
  • Toggle Reduce Transparency in iOS Settings: every pill's blur must be replaced by a solid surface.
