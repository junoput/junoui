What to check

  • Home indicator. Base and --fixed pad themselves with env(safe-area-inset-bottom); the floating variants add it to their bottom margin instead (offset off the edge, not max()). Either way, no label may sit under the bar.
  • Tap targets. Every item is at least 44px tall on a coarse pointer. Try hitting the outermost item with a thumb.
  • Blur. --float and --pill use backdrop-filter with the -webkit- prefix first. Scroll content beneath them: it should blur. Turn on Reduce Transparency — the surface must go solid.
  • Tap highlight. No grey square should flash outside the round bubble on --pill / --icon.
  • Landscape. Rotate: the bar must not run under the notch on either side.
