What to check

  • Height. On a coarse pointer --juno-size-tap-min is promoted from 24px to 44px, so even --sm buttons must be thumb-sized here. The dashed band below is exactly 44px for comparison.
  • Tap highlight. Press and hold: no grey iOS rectangle should escape the control's own rounded shape.
  • Text selection. A long press on a button must not start a text selection or pop the callout menu.
  • Double-tap. Tapping twice quickly must not zoom the page.
